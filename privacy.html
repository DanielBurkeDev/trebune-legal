IrishSpotSesh

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 July 2026

IrishSpotSesh is an app for discovering Irish skateparks, street spots, shops and events, and for planning skate sessions with friends. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, and what your rights are. It is written to be read by humans — if anything is unclear, email us.

1. Who we are

The data controller for IrishSpotSesh is Daniel Burke, an independent developer based in Ireland.

Privacy contact: skatdev+privacy@gmail.com

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Email address, username and password when you create an account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Clerk) and are never visible to us.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email, name and profile picture from Google.
  • An optional profile photo (avatar) if you choose to upload one.

Content you create

  • Check-ins at skateparks, spots and shops.
  • Favourites, star ratings and likes.
  • Skate sessions you plan: session name, scheduled time, the venues on your route, notes, and any message you attach when sharing a session with a friend.
  • Friend connections (requests you send and accept) and trick lists you create or share.

Location

Your location is never stored on our servers.

If you grant permission, the app reads your approximate location, on-device and only while you're using the app, to: sort spots by distance ("Near Me"), preview a route to a venue, and show local weather. We request balanced (not highest-precision) accuracy and never access location in the background.

The one case where coordinates leave your device: to fetch a weather forecast, your approximate latitude/longitude is sent to the weather services Met Éireann and Open-Meteo. These requests are not tied to your account.

Crash and performance data

If the app crashes or misbehaves, a technical report (stack trace, device model, OS version, app version) is sent to Sentry, hosted in the EU. We have configured Sentry so that it does not receive your IP address, email or device identifiers, and crash reports are not linked to your account.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising or analytics SDKs, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No background location, no location history, no contacts, no message content scanning.
  • We do not sell personal data to anyone.

3. Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the service (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) — contract): your account, favourites, check-ins, sessions, friends and everything else you'd expect the app to do.
  • With your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): location features. You can decline or revoke the permission at any time in your device settings and the app keeps working (with distance sorting and local weather disabled).
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): crash reporting to keep the app stable, and basic security of our systems.

5. What other users can see

IrishSpotSesh has social features. Your username and avatar are visible to other users. Your check-ins can appear to others at the same venue and in your friends' activity feed. A skate session is visible to friends only if you mark it discoverable or share it. Friend requests share your username and avatar with the person you send them to. Think of these the way you would any social app: don't put anything in a username, avatar or session note that you wouldn't want other skaters to see.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and content: kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile and content are deleted from our backend.
  • Crash data: retained by Sentry for its standard rolling window (approximately 90 days), then deleted automatically.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy of it (portability).
  • Correct your data — you can edit your username and avatar directly in the app.
  • Delete your data — use Profile → Delete Account in the app, or email us and we'll do it for you.
  • Withdraw consent for location at any time in your device settings.
  • Object to or restrict processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) or your local EU supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these, email skatdev+privacy@gmail.com. We'll respond within one month.

8. International transfers

Some of our providers (section 4) are US companies. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, it is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, as implemented in each provider's data processing agreement.

9. Children

IrishSpotSesh is not directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored in your device's secure keychain/keystore. Passwords are hashed by Clerk and never stored by us.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects (for example, when new features ship), we will update this page and the effective date above, and flag significant changes in the app.
